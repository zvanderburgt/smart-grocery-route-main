Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

The short version. Breadcrumb keeps your grocery list on the device you use it on. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. The only time your list leaves the device is when you turn on Google Sheets sync — and then it goes to a spreadsheet in your own Google account, not to ours. Photo scanning is the one optional feature that sends an image to a third party, and only when you tap scan.

Breadcrumb is a grocery-list app operated by Zack VanderBurgt ("Breadcrumb", "we", "us") as a personal, household deployment at breadcrumbapp.netlify.app. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It applies to that site and to the same app installed to a home screen or shown inside a Home Assistant dashboard.

1. What stays on your device

Breadcrumb stores its data in your browser's local storage for that site. Nothing in this section is uploaded anywhere. It includes:

  • your grocery items, quantities, categories and checked state;
  • section names and the order you arranged them in;
  • favourites, saved recipes, learned brands and learned store categories;
  • shopping-trip history, used to suggest the route you usually walk;
  • display and behaviour settings;
  • if you use the optional photo scanner: which OCR provider you picked, and any API key you paste in for it;
  • if this deployment's sync uses the relay: a sync token that lets the app talk to the relay, kept for up to 90 days.

You can remove all of it at any time from your browser (clear site data for breadcrumbapp.netlify.app), or from the app itself with Settings → Backup & Restore. Export a backup first if you want to keep the list.

2. Google Sheets sync (optional, off by default)

Sync copies your list into a Google Sheets spreadsheet that you choose, so several devices can share one list. It uses Google's spreadsheets scope, which allows reading and writing the spreadsheets you grant access to. The app only touches the one spreadsheet ID you enter.

What is written to the sheet: item names, quantities, categories, checked state, section order and sync timestamps. Nothing else is sent — no contacts, no email content, no location.

There are two ways this deployment can perform the sync:

  • Direct sign-in. You authorise in the browser and the app calls Google's Sheets API with a short-lived access token that lives in that tab's session storage and is discarded when the tab closes.
  • Relay. A Netlify Function on this site holds a Google refresh token supplied once by the person who set up the deployment, and forwards the same requests. This exists because Google will not run its sign-in screen inside a dashboard iframe or an Android webview. The refresh token is stored as an environment variable in the hosting account, never in the page. If a shared password is configured, the relay asks for it before it will serve data.

In both cases the list is stored in the Google account that authorised the sync. We do not keep a separate copy of your list. The contents of the spreadsheet are also governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy. You can remove the data by clearing rows in the sheet, deleting the sheet, or revoking the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google user data and Limited Use

Breadcrumb's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In practice: Google user data is used only to provide the sync feature you asked for, is not transferred to anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not read by any person except where you ask for support and share it.

3. Photo scanning (optional)

When you scan a photo or a screenshot of a list, the image is sent to the OCR provider selected in Settings for text extraction. The two options are OpenRouter and OpenCode. The image goes there and back; Breadcrumb does not store it on any server, and the scan result becomes list items on your device. Those providers process requests under their own policies, which may include short-term logging. Don't scan anything you would not send to that provider.

Barcode scanning is different: it looks the code up in a list bundled with the app on your device and sends nothing anywhere.

4. Voice input

Voice entry uses the speech recognition built into your browser. In most browsers that means the audio is processed by the browser vendor's speech service (Chrome sends it to Google). Breadcrumb receives only the resulting text. If you don't use voice entry, no microphone access is requested.

5. Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted on Netlify, and its server-side features run as Netlify Functions. Like any web host, Netlify records standard request logs — IP address, browser user agent, requested URL and time — and keeps them under Netlify's privacy policy. The app also installs a service worker so it can open offline; that caches the app's own files on your device and holds no personal data of its own.

Breadcrumb sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics, pixels or advertising identifiers. Google sets its own cookies on Google's own domain while you sign in; that part is Google's, not ours.

6. What we don't do

  • no user accounts, no passwords for Breadcrumb itself;
  • no analytics, session recording, heatmaps or crash reporting services;
  • no advertising and no advertising identifiers;
  • no selling, renting or sharing of your data with data brokers;
  • no marketing email — the only email address involved is the one you write to us at.

7. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • On your device: until you clear it. Settings → Backup & Restore can wipe the list, and clearing site data removes everything including settings.
  • In your spreadsheet: until you delete the rows or the file. Deleting the list in the app propagates to the sheet on the next sync.
  • Deployment credentials: the relay's Google refresh token and any shared password live in this site's environment variables. Ask us and we will delete or rotate them; you can also revoke the token yourself at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which makes relay sync stop working until a new one is minted.
  • Host logs: retained for the short period Netlify keeps them, then discarded.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Sync tokens are kept in the browser's own storage rather than in a URL or a cookie, and the relay checks who is calling before it answers. No system is perfectly secure, so treat the spreadsheet as the place your list is shared: anyone with access to that Google account or that sheet can read it.

9. Children

Breadcrumb is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. The app has no accounts and no server-side profiles, so there is nothing to delete beyond the data on the device.

10. Changes to this policy

If what the app does changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top is updated. Material changes will be noted in the app. Continuing to use Breadcrumb after a change means the updated policy applies.
