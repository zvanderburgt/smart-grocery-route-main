3. Your list, your data, your backups

You own the content you put into Breadcrumb. You are responsible for it, including anything you scan, dictate or sync.

List data lives in your browser's storage on each device. That means clearing site data, using private browsing, switching browsers or losing a device can lose your list, and a sync that is misconfigured can overwrite a list with an older one. The app has an export/restore feature in Settings → Backup & Restore; use it before anything you would not want to redo by hand.

When sync is on, the shared copy lives in a Google Sheets spreadsheet in the authorising Google account. Who else can read that sheet is controlled by that Google account, not by us.
